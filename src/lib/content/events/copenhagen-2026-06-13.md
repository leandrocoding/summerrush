Join us on Saturday, June 13, 2026 for a Hide + Seek game across Copenhagen and the surrounding area. With Denmark's extensive public transport network, expect quick connections, tactical station choices, and plenty of opportunities to move around the city.

### Getting There

* **Meeting point:** Copenhagen Central Station (København H)
* **Flights:** Copenhagen Airport (CPH) is about 15 minutes from the city center by train or metro.
* **Trains:** Copenhagen H has direct connections across Denmark, Sweden, and Germany.
* **Transit tickets:** Consider a City Pass, Rejsekort, or another suitable day ticket.

### Game Info & Map

* **Format:** Standard Hide + Seek rules with a medium metric setup.
* **Starting location:** Copenhagen H
* **Meet up time:** 09:00 CEST
* **End time:** 19:00 CEST
* **Duration:** ~10 hours (depends when we finish two rounds)
* **Hiding radius:** 500 m around the chosen station.
