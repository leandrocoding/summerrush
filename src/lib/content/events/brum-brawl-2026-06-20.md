Brum Brawl is a new, but JLTG inspired, team game format taking place across Birmingham, open to new and experienced players. Teams claim different areas on the map by visiting them and then drawing challenges from a deck. Similar to many actual series of JLTG, the strategy element of the game lies in:

* Planning an efficient route between locations
* Making effective use of any special cards obtained from the deck, such as curses
* Working out inventive ways to best complete the challenges

### Getting There

* **Meeting point:** To be confirmed
* **Flights:** Birmingham Airport (BHX) is well served across all of Europe, and is about 10 minutes away from the city centre by train. 
* **Trains:** Birmingham is well served throughout the UK with direct connections to major airports and cities including London, Manchester, Edinburgh and Glasgow.
* **Transit tickets:** The Nexus network will be used.

### Game Info & Map

* **Format:** A brand new area claiming game!
