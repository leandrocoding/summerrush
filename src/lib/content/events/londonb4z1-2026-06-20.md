Join us on Saturday, June 20, 2026 for a Battle across London. You will flght to claim tube stations and then baltle in a range of challanges to keep hold of them, or to steal thoes of your oponents. The game uses zone 1 and 2 of the London undergroumnd, and only uses the acceible stations so anyone can join!

### Getting There

- **Meeting point:** We will meet at Russell Square, at 9am to begin the game as promptly as possible. It is easily reached by the Tube from anywhere in London.
- **Flights:** London has 6 airports with flights from just about anywhere.
- **Trains:** The Eurostar runs direct into London, with possibilities on the morning of the event. You can also take trains to London easily from anywhere in the UK
- **Transit tickets:** The game will run on the tap in tap out system of the underground. You can also buy a day pass if desiered but this is slightly more costly.

### Game Info & Map

- **Format:** A battle across Multiple stations. Each station will have a challange apended to it, and the first team to do the challange will claim the station. The claim can allways be challanged by a battle though, and if you suceed at the battle, you can steal others stations. The team with the most station at the end of the game day will be crowned winner.
- **Playable area:** Zone 1 and 2 of the london underground.
