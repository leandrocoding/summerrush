** *Event Date: 21.08.2026* **

Be part of a thrilling hitchhiking competition: Race to Ben's Playground.

The objective of the event is simple: The team which arrives at Ben's famous playground in Merlischachen, Switzerland in the shortest time by hitchhiking, wins!

### About the routes

From Utrecht to Merlischachen, there are 3 principal routes expected to have the most traffic:
* **East:** Going to Germany and following the Rhine river up until arriving in Basel, Switzerland.
* **South:** Going to Belgium and travelling via Luxembourg and Strasbourg to enter Switzerland in Basel.
* **Middle:** Going via Maastricht and Saarbrücken to end up in Strasbourg, following the Rhine river to Basel.

Teams are free to decide whichever route they want to take, even a custom route if they desire.

### Getting There

* **Meeting point:** Neude Square in Utrecht on Friday, 21st of August at 09:30 AM. The competition officially starts at 10:00 AM.
* **Destination:** The playground Ben hid in Season 7 in Merlischachen, Switzerland. The deadline to arrive is Saturday at 21:00.
* **Public Transport Apps:** It is recommended to download local ticket apps, especially the DB Navigator app, to quickly buy eTickets if public transport is needed in big cities or emergencies.

### Game Info

* **Format:** A two-day hitchhiking race. Teams are forced to take a mandatory 12-hour rest period. You can start this rest period between 17:30 and 22:00.
* **Teams:** Everybody is required to partner up (nobody will hitchhike alone, maximum 3 people per group). During the entire competition, teams must stay together and cannot split up.
* **Movement:** Teams are only allowed to take hitchhiking offers from vehicles they or their family/friends do not own. Public transport is permitted only in big cities to reach good hitchhiking spots. Hitchhiking next to a highway is strictly prohibited!
* **Challenges:** A custom map with secret challenges at specific locations will be provided. Depending on the difficulty and distance from the optimal path, completing these challenges grants time reductions.
* **Preparation & Safety:** An emergency fund (~€50) is recommended. Every team must share their Live Location with the Helpdesk all day and must have at least one fully charged power bank. Trust your gut feeling when entering cars and discreetly text license plates to the Helpdesk.

### Game Map & Resources

We highly recommend using the **Hitchhiking Map** to find spots across the world. Aim for green dots on the map with a thick black border, as those are reviewed the most positively. The map also reveals the average waiting time at each spot.
